01 About the conference

Materials, design, engineering and medicine, in one room.

SBRS ICON 2027 will bring together researchers, clinicians, scientists, engineers, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, and policymakers from around the world to advance sustainable solutions for the future of healthcare. The conference will connect materials innovation, engineering design, and biomanufacturing with next-generation medical technologies to address global healthcare challenges while promoting environmental sustainability and planetary well-being.

What this conference sets out to do.

    Catalyse sustainable innovations for next-generation healthcare

  2. OBJ-02

    Foster a global ecosystem for collaborative research and sustainable biomanufacturing

    Bridge materials, design, engineering, and medicine through interdisciplinary partnerships

  4. OBJ-04

    Accelerate the translation of research into impactful healthcare solutions

  5. OBJ-05

    Advance technologies that benefit both people and the planet
